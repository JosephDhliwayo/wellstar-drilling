Who We Are

Zimbabwe's Water Access Partner

Wellstar Drilling was founded with a simple mission: bring safe, reliable water to every corner of Zimbabwe. From remote rural farms to urban commercial properties, we have delivered sustainable water solutions for over 5 years.

Our team of certified hydrogeologists, skilled drillers, and pump engineers uses modern drilling rigs and equipment to locate and access groundwater efficiently and responsibly. We are fully registered with the Zimbabwe National Water Authority (ZINWA) and adhere to all industry standards.

What We Do

Comprehensive Water Solutions

From site survey to running water — we handle every step of the process.

Borehole Drilling

Professional rotary and percussion drilling for residential, agricultural, and commercial properties to reach reliable groundwater aquifers.

Pump Installation

Supply and installation of submersible and surface pumps, solar-powered pump systems, and pump controls tailored to your yield and budget.

Water Quality Testing

Laboratory and on-site testing for bacteria, pH, minerals, and contaminants. Receive a full report and recommendations to ensure water is safe for drinking and irrigation.

Hydrogeological Survey

Expert ground surveys using geophysical methods to identify the best drilling sites, maximising water yield and minimising dry borehole risk.

Borehole Rehabilitation

Restore an old or under-performing borehole through development, airlifting, and re-equipping to bring it back to full productivity.

Solar Pump Systems

Off-grid solar-powered water pumping for farms and remote communities — reducing electricity costs and ensuring water access even without grid power.
